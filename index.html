Who We Are

A youth-led platform bridging global commitments to local action.

The WFF Rwanda Chapter operates under the global World Food Forum framework hosted by FAO. We connect young people across Rwanda to opportunities, knowledge, and networks that drive real change in food systems.

Vision

A thriving Rwandan food system that is inclusive, innovative, and beneficial for both people and planet.

Mission

To mobilize and empower young Rwandans to lead sustainable transformation in agriculture and food systems.

What We Do
Six groups. One mission.
Each thematic group focuses on a critical area of Rwanda’s food systems. Find yours.
01

Climate-Smart Crop Production

Resilient farming, soil health, sustainable irrigation, improved seeds, and agroecological practices.

02

Nutrition Security & Food Systems

Youth-led nutrition education, school feeding linkages, food safety standards, and fortification pilots.

03

Agribusiness & Market Systems

Entrepreneurship, value chains, market access, post-harvest loss reduction, and youth-led branding.

04

Sustainable Livestock & Animal Resources

Animal health, dairy and poultry value addition, small ruminants, and veterinary service access.

05

Digital Solutions & Agri-Tech

Mobile apps, data-driven farming, AI in agriculture, and youth-run innovation hubs.

06

Climate Action & Natural Resources

Community agroecology, watershed management, renewable energy, and climate policy advocacy.
